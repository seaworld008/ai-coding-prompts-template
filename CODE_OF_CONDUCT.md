# Code of Conduct

## 中文说明

这个仓库欢迎理性、友好、愿意共同打磨内容的人参与。

我们希望这里保持几个基本氛围：

- 尊重不同背景和经验水平的贡献者
- 讨论问题时聚焦内容质量，而不是攻击个人
- 鼓励指出风险、漏洞和不足，但尽量给出建设性表达
- 接受不同写法风格，但优先服从仓库整体结构和可维护性

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment:

- being respectful and constructive
- giving actionable feedback
- discussing ideas in good faith
- helping improve clarity, accuracy, and maintainability
- welcoming learners and first-time contributors

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- personal attacks or harassment
- insulting, dismissive, or intentionally hostile language
- discriminatory remarks
- spam, low-effort abuse, or repeated disruptive behavior
- publishing harmful or obviously unsafe content without clear warning or context

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project interactions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, lock, or decline content that clearly harms the health of the repository or its contributors.

The goal is to keep the project practical, welcoming, and useful.
